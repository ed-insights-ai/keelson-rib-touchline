# Preseason Scouting Questionnaire

What the report must answer, for the coaching staff of the focus programme.
Every question is answered from the digest first; the news layer adds only
verified, cited context the record cannot hold. This file is the report's
content contract — tune it here, not in code.

## The conference landscape (once)

- L1. How did the conference actually stack up last season — record, goal
  difference, and conference-only form for every programme, in one table a
  coach reads in ten seconds?
- L2. Who returns the most production and who lost the most? Rank programmes
  by returning share of goals and minutes. Name the biggest single departure
  in the conference.
- L3. Given L1 and L2 together, which programmes are rising, which are
  exposed, and where does the focus programme honestly sit?
- L4. What changed in the offseason that the record cannot see — coaching
  changes, notable arrivals, programmes trending in preseason polls?
  (News layer: cited or absent.)

## Per opponent (one section each, in schedule order vs the focus)

- O1. **Form**: last season's overall and conference record, goal
  difference, clean sheets, times shut out, one-goal games. Multi-season
  trend if held.
- O2. **How and when they score**: goals by half and by 15-minute band —
  do they start fast, finish strong, live off overtime? Same for goals
  conceded: when are they vulnerable?
- O3. **Pressure profile**: shots and shots-on-goal for and against,
  corners for and against, fouls committed and drawn. Do they generate
  chances or absorb them?
- O4. **Discipline**: yellow/red totals — does their game invite set
  pieces and advantages?
- O5. **Goalkeeping**: who kept goal, minutes, save percentage; does the
  keeper return this season?
- O6. **Roster turnover**: who walked out the door and what did they take
  (share of goals, assists, points, minutes departing vs returning)?
  Name the departed leaders. How large is the newcomer class?
- O7. **Returning threats**: the returning players a coach will ask about,
  ranked by last season's production, each with a career line — is the
  trajectory rising?
- O8. **Head-to-head**: the all-time held record vs the focus, current
  streak, and the last meeting's story.
- O9. **This season**: when and where the focus plays them.
- O10. **What the record can't tell you**: new head coach, transfers with
  no collected history, summer developments. (News layer: cited or absent —
  an explicit "no verified news" is a valid and useful answer.)
- O11. **The takeaway**: two or three match-preparation hypotheses that
  follow *from the cited numbers above* — phrased as testable preparation
  points, never as certainties.

## The focus programme (once, honest)

- F1. The same form, patterns, turnover, and returning-threat treatment,
  written as a self-assessment: what does the record say we were, what
  walked back in the door, and what do the numbers say we must fix?
- F2. **The summer ledger**: what did our own roster do between seasons that
  the public record can verify — summer-league minutes (USL League Two,
  NPSL, UPSL, The League for Clubs), national or youth-national call-ups,
  honors, notable club activity? Search club-first (fetch the rosters of
  geographically plausible summer clubs and cross-reference names), then
  player-first — and for every rostered player, run the BARE NAME as its own
  query, not only name+league combinations: league award posts and social
  announcements surface under the plain name when structured queries miss
  them entirely. When a hit is found, immediately search the player's name
  plus that league for honors (all-conference, Best XI, weekly awards) —
  membership found means performance evidence may exist too. Source classes, in confidence order: league/club official
  pages and local news (high); club social announcements when capturable at
  a stable URL (medium); a player's own public posts about their soccer
  activity (medium, soccer-relevant only — never personal life). Only
  findings verified at a source make the ledger; an empty summer renders as
  nothing, never as filler.

## The watch list (once)

- W1. The conference's top returning scorers and creators in one table —
  the players who will decide games against us.
