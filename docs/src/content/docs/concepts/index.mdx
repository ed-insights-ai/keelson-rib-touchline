This tier explains why the rib is built the way it is. It assumes you know the
[Keelson](https://danielscholl.github.io/keelson/) harness and spends its words
on what Touchline adds. Every page anchors its claims to a file and a symbol in
the shipped code, and every page ends by naming the lab that makes it physical.

Read in order and the tier is a course: how pages are collected under proof,
parsed against hostile templates, reconciled into canonical identities, computed
into one deterministic digest, and interpreted by a model that is never allowed
to invent a number. Four pages carry the deepest transferable ideas and are
marked as the flagships of the spine: identity, absence, the determinism
boundary, and evidence.
