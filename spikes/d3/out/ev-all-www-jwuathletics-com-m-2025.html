Wildcat Athletics

JWU Wildcat Athletics helps students grow in and out of the classroom. Through varsity sports, intramurals, fitness and wellness programs, we help students build skills for professional success and personal growth.

In our 30+ year history, our Wildcats have made 84 NCAA tournament appearances, including a national championship win for Men’s Wrestling in 2025. They have also captured nine regional championships and 98 conference championships. In the 2025-2026 athletic season, 213 Wildcats were named to the Academic All-Conference team, among many other accolades.

Our Providence Campus is home to 18 DIII varsity teams, multiple NCAA Division III All-American and Conference of New England (CNE) award recipients.

    JWU Wins First-Ever NCAA National Wrestling Championship

    March 21, 2025

    On March 14-15, Johnson & Wales took center stage in college wrestling, hosting the NCAA Division III Wrestling National Championship at the Amica Mutual Pavilion in Providence, Rhode Island. For the first time in over 30 years, the tournament returned to New England, and JWU made history tying Wartburg College for the national title.
